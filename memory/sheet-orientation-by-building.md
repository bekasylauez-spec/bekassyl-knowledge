User rule, 31.10.2026, Резервуар КЖ: «здание формасы вертикальный листке ұқсайды. здание вертикалдан созылса вертикальный лист қой. ал гор созылса гор лист қой».

The plan was 15000×18000 (taller than wide) and I had put it on a landscape A3, leaving the right half of the sheet empty.

**Why:** a landscape sheet for a tall plan wastes half the sheet. The analog does the same thing: the spec sheets 3.8 and 3.10 are portrait.

**How to apply:** before laying out any plan sheet, compare the plan's height and width, including the dims and axes. If it is taller than wide, use a portrait format; if wider, landscape. Put the spec and notes in the leftover band, above the stamp.
